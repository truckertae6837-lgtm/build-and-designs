LadderLevel Wedge Pair

Interlocking polymer wedges that stabilize ladder feet on slopes, gravel, lawn, and jobsite uneven ground — without stacking scrap wood.

What it does & how

Two matching wedges with ratchet teeth on the mating faces. Slide until the top faces are coplanar and level under each ladder foot. High-friction top pad grips the ladder shoe; bottom tread grips ground.

  • Works as a pair — one or both sides depending on slope.
  • Teeth lock under load; pull-tab releases.
  • Bright color for visibility on site.

Step-by-step home build

  1. Open ladder_level_A.scad → render → export wedge_left.stl / wedge_right.stl / pad.stl.
  2. Slice wedges: 0.2mm, 5+ walls, 40–60% gyroid, PETG 250°C / 80–90°C bed. Print flat on largest face.
  3. Slice TPU pads soft; glue to top recess after IPA wipe.
  4. Deburr teeth with 220 grit — teeth must mesh without binding dry.
  5. Functional test on 5–12° plywood ramp with ladder empty, then with 150 lb sandbag on bottom rung (controlled).
  6. Mark max slope on product card (e.g. “≤12° residential”).
  7. Photograph on real uneven ground for ads / patent appendix.
Safety: This is a leveling aid, not a substitute for ladder manufacturer instructions, tie-offs, or 3-point contact. Do not market as OSHA-certified until you fund independent testing.

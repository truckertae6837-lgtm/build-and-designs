How to use these patent packs

  1. Complete inventor disclosure with your legal name and dates.
  2. Attach photos, CAD, and demo video of your actual prototype.
  3. Run a prior-art search on Google Patents; note closest hits.
  4. Hire a USPTO-registered patent attorney/agent for provisional filing.
  5. After filing, you may market more openly while the 12-month clock runs.

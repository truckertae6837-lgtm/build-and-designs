What it does

  • Reads soil moisture at bottom, middle, and top zones of the pot/root ball.
  • Classifies under-hydrated / OK / over-hydrated per zone and overall.
  • App camera: identify plant species/cultivar class, then recommend water, nutrients/food, or both.
  • Push/local alerts when a zone stays dry or waterlogged beyond thresholds.

CAD that matches the photo

  • Teal accent ring at the neck (print in teal filament)
  • Flat-face stem with rounded sides
  • Three recessed oval sensor windows (top / mid / bot)
  • Conical soil tip + hollow wire channel into the head

How it works (system)

  1. Three capacitive sensing pads seated in the oval windows along the stem.
  2. MCU (ESP32-C3) in the capsule head samples ADC, filters noise, estimates moisture proxy.
  3. BLE/Wi‑Fi sends readings to the phone app every N seconds.
  4. App runs plant ID from a photo, then merges species profile + 3-zone state → action card.
Do not use bare copper resistive probes long-term — they corrode and poison soil. Prefer capacitive sensors with conformal coating / food-safe epoxy.

Step-by-step home build

  1. Pick your size — S / M / L from the chart above based on pot depth.
  2. Get the STL — Download the matching plant_sense_X.stl, or open cad/plant_sense_probe.scad, set SIZE, Render (F6), Export STL. For teal ring: export PART="ring" separately (or use the print-plate STL and paint/swap filament).
  3. Slice & print — PETG, 0.2 mm, 4 walls, 25% infill. Stem upright. Head upright on its base. Ring flat on the bed in teal.
  4. Prep sensors — Trim three capacitive pads to fit the oval windows. Extend silicone wires long enough to reach the head cavity through the hollow stem.
  5. Seat pads — Press pads into the three ovals (bot / mid / top). Epoxy edges only; leave sensing face clean. Cure 24h.
  6. Pull wires — Feed wires up the hollow channel, out the tenon into the head.
  7. Slide teal ring — Onto the stem tenon until it seats under the head socket.
  8. Wire ESP32 — Pads → ADC pins. Battery → TP4056 → 3V3. USB-C on the board aligns with the head cutout.
  9. Close head — Seat head on tenon (CA or press fit). Snap/glue rear lid. Leave USB accessible.
  10. Flash & calibrate — Upload firmware. Dry air = 0% proxy; soaked sponge = 100%. Store in NVS.
  11. App — Pair BLE, photograph plant, confirm water / feed recommendation.

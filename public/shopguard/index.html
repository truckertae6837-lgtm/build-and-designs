ShopGuard

Solo-worker wearable that detects falls and prolonged no-motion (possible loss of consciousness), sounds a local alarm, and notifies your circle. Large push for SOS. Push to cancel during countdown.

Controls (must feel obvious with gloves)

  • Arm / Power: slide switch or long-press.
  • SOS (push for alarm): large recessed button or side slider — starts immediate siren + alert.
  • Cancel: same button short-press during 15–30s countdown after auto-detect, or dedicated cancel.
  • LED: green armed · amber countdown · red alarming.
  • No mandatory subscription — SMS via phone BLE bridge or optional LTE SKU later.

How it detects “fell / not conscious”

  1. IMU (MPU6050 / BMI160) samples accel + gyro ~50–100 Hz.
  2. Fall heuristic: free-fall-like low-g → high impact → stillness.
  3. No-motion: while armed, motion energy below threshold for N minutes (configurable 2–10).
  4. Enter countdown with beep; user cancels if OK.
  5. If not cancelled: local 95–105 dB siren + notify contacts via phone app.
Market as a personal alert aid, not a certified medical/life-safety device, until you complete UL/IEC and clinical validation.

Step-by-step home build

  1. Print shopguard_A_clip.scad parts (body, lid, clip, button cap).
  2. Heat-set M2 inserts; dry-fit IMU + MCU + battery.
  3. Solder: IMU I²C → ESP32; piezo via transistor; buttons to GPIOs with pull-ups; LED.
  4. Flash firmware from /products/shopguard/firmware/.
  5. Calibrate fall thresholds on foam mat (do not injure yourself testing).
  6. Verify countdown cancel works with gloves on.
  7. Pair phone app; set 2 emergency contacts; test SMS/push in controlled way.
  8. Record demo video for PoC / patent appendix.
